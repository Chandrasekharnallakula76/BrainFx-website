Cognitive Function is Critical to Comprehensive, Holistic Care

Easily set baselines and track brain health in under 15 minutes

Time is precious in the clinic. In under 15 minutes, get an indication of individual cognitive strengths and challenges to build a reference point for cognitive health to help monitor over time and to determine whether more tests are needed.

Deliver a better patient experience with personalized cognitive care

Patients are demanding better, more personalized care. Build a comprehensive profile of your patients' cognitive function and brain health, then use advanced analytics to compare their results with tailored, best-match cohorts. By understanding each patient's unique cognitive strengths and challenges, you can deliver more targeted, personalized care designed specifically for their needs.

Earlier detection for preventative, lower cost care

Healthier patients and lower costs start with earlier detection. Traditional cognitive tests tend to miss higher-order skills that are the first/earliest to be impacted by changes in cognition. Detect changes in cognitive function sooner by assessing your patients complex cognitive skills through real-world, function-based activities.

More time caring, less time scoring and reporting

Spend more time caring and less time scoring and reporting.

BrainFx assessment results are automatically scored and reporting is immediately available. Every report comes with detailed results from each assessment activity, with standardized scoring and percentiles. Comparison of scores & percentiles is automatically matched to pre-selected reference population.

Get precise insights with our post-assessment tools, and leverage our clinical decision support resources to plan highly targeted next steps and treatment.

Make clear, confident treatment and next steps decisions

Better care and more revenue. There doesn’t have to be trade-offs.

Important Information: BrainFx Assessments only provide information about neurofunctional performance. Assessment results detail the patient/client's performance on assessment activities completed and only assist the health care team in identifying potential areas of strength or challenge as related to function. Assessments DO NOT provide any diagnosis or treatment recommendations. BrainFx Assessments are only permitted to be delivered by trained clinicians. Assessment reports are not stand-alone and require the interpretation of a health professional within their scope of practice and in the context of all information that they have gathered about their patient/client. In Canada, BrainFx Assessments are cleared by Health Canada as a Class 1 Software as a Medical Device. BrainFx Assessments and the statements made on this website have not been evaluated by any other regulatory bodies. Assessments are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Information provided by BrainFx is not a substitute for face-to-face consultation with your health care professional and should not be construed as individual medical advice.
