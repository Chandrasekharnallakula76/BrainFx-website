About HappNeuron Pro

HappNeuron Pro is a digital cognitive training platform used by clinicians to help patients build upon or rebuild core cognitive functions.

Its structured exercise library targets attention, memory, executive function, language, and processing speed. Programs are designed to be engaging and clinically relevant so patients can practice real-world skills and support measurable improvement over time.

When paired with BrainFx, training can be aligned with a patient’s functional cognitive profile—supporting more targeted intervention and clearer outcome tracking.

About BrainFx

BrainFx is a digital neurofunctional assessment platform designed to detect mild to moderate cognitive dysfunction that traditional tests often miss.

Through interactive, real-world activities, BrainFx measures functional cognitive skills and generates immediate reporting that supports clinical interpretation, targeted care planning, and longitudinal monitoring. Together, BrainFx and HappNeuron Pro create a more complete model for cognitive assessment and intervention.
